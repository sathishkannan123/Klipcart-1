Returns & Refunds

Hassle-free 30-day returns. Your satisfaction is our priority.

How to Return an Item

1

Request

Go to 'Your Orders' and select the item to return.

2

Pack

Keep the item in its original packaging with tags.

3

Pickup

Our delivery partner will pick it up from your address.

4

Refund

Money will be refunded to your original payment method.

rule

30-Day Return Window

You have 30 days from the date of delivery to initiate a return. To be eligible for a return, your item must be unused, in the same condition that you received it, and in its original packaging along with the receipt or proof of purchase.

credit_score

Refund Processing Time

Once we receive your item, we will inspect it and notify you that we have received your returned item. If your return is approved, we will initiate a refund to your original method of payment.

  • UPI / Wallets: 1 - 2 Business Days
  • Credit / Debit Cards: 5 - 7 Business Days
  • Cash on Delivery: Transferred to your provided Bank Account in 3 - 5 Business Days.
warning

Non-Returnable Items

Certain types of items cannot be returned due to hygiene and safety reasons. These include:

  • Perishable goods (e.g., food, flowers).
  • Personal care items (e.g., innerwear, beauty products).
  • Gift cards and downloadable software products.
