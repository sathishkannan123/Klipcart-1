Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. Learn how we protect your data.

Last Updated: March 2026

At Klipcart Inc., we are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. If you have any questions or concerns about our policy, or our practices with regards to your personal information, please contact us at privacy@klipcart.com.

person_search 1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when registering at the website, expressing an interest in obtaining information about us or our products, or otherwise contacting us. The personal information we collect includes:

  • Personal Details: Name, email address, phone numbers, and delivery address.
  • Payment Data: We collect data necessary to process your payment if you make purchases. (Note: Payment details are securely processed by our payment partners like Stripe/Razorpay).
  • Log and Usage Data: Service-related, diagnostic, usage, and performance information our servers automatically collect when you access or use our Website.

database 2. How We Use Your Information

We use personal information collected via our Website for a variety of business purposes described below:

  • To fulfill and manage your orders, payments, returns, and exchanges.
  • To facilitate account creation and logon process.
  • To send you administrative information, such as order confirmations and shipping updates.
  • To protect our Services (e.g., fraud monitoring and prevention).

cookie 3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We may use cookies and similar tracking technologies (like web beacons and pixels) to access or store information. Specific information about how we use such technologies and how you can refuse certain cookies is set out in our Cookie Notice.

security 4. Data Security

We have implemented appropriate technical and organizational security measures designed to protect the security of any personal information we process. However, please also remember that we cannot guarantee that the internet itself is 100% secure. Although we will do our best to protect your personal information, transmission of personal information to and from our Website is at your own risk.
