Frequently Asked Questions

How do I track my Klipcart order?

expand_more

You can track your order by going to the Your Orders page. Click on the specific order to see the real-time delivery status.

What is your return policy?

expand_more

We offer a 30-day hassle-free return policy. If you are not satisfied with your product, you can request a return from your orders page, provided the product is unused and in its original packaging.

How long does delivery take?

expand_more

Standard delivery takes 3-5 business days depending on your location. Metro cities usually receive orders within 24-48 hours via our express delivery network.

My payment failed but money was deducted. What should I do?

expand_more

Don't worry! If money was deducted for a failed transaction, it will be automatically refunded to your original payment method within 5-7 business days by your bank.
